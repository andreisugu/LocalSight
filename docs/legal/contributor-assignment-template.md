## 1. Assignment

The Contributor assigns to the Maintainer all right, title and interest worldwide in the
Contribution, including copyright and any database rights, for the full term of those
rights. The Maintainer may license, sublicense, modify, distribute and commercialise the
Contribution, including under the Business Source License 1.1 and separate commercial
terms.

## 2. Licence back

The Maintainer grants the Contributor a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free
licence to use the Contribution for non-commercial portfolio and educational purposes,
provided the Contributor does not grant third parties rights that conflict with this
assignment.

## 3. Moral rights

To the extent permitted by law, the Contributor waives, and agrees not to assert, moral
rights in the Contribution against the Maintainer or the Maintainer's licensees. Where
waiver is not permitted, the Contributor consents to the uses described in this document.

## 4. Representations

The Contributor confirms that:

- the Contribution was written by the Contributor, or the Contributor has all rights
  needed to assign it;
- it does not contain code or assets under AGPL, GPL, LGPL or another copyleft licence
  that would conflict with the Project's licensing;
- it does not contain confidential information, trade secrets or third-party material
  without written permission;
- no employer, university or other party has rights that conflict with this assignment.
